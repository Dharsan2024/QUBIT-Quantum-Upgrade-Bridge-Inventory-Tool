# QUBIT Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want QUBIT to be a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous open-source community. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project spaces is expected to act in good faith and treat others with dignity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful, especially when discussing security or research claims.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives.
- Challenge ideas, evidence, and implementations without attacking people.
- Respect confidentiality. Do not share private repositories, credentials, personal data, or embargoed findings.
- Accept feedback and help correct mistakes openly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information, secrets, exploit details, or sensitive source code without permission.
- Misrepresenting results, plagiarising work, or presenting unverified claims as evidence.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors to bypass security, review, licensing, or validation safeguards.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `dharsanlingadurai24@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may ask for clarification, issue a warning, remove content, limit participation, or ban a participant when necessary to protect the community.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is not acceptable.
